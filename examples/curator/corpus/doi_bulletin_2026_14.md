## Page 1

This bulletin updates statistical reporting requirements for commercial liability insurers writing business in the State.

Insurers should review the requirements below with their statistical agents before the next reporting cycle.

## Page 2

The Department has observed growth in claims involving environmental contaminants and emerging chemical exposures.

Consistent reporting across insurers allows the Department to monitor loss trends and market availability.

## Page 3

Effective January 1, 2027, insurers shall code claims alleging bodily injury or property damage arising from per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) under a separate cause-of-loss code, and report them separately in the annual data call.

Claims already open on that date should be recoded at their next reserve review.

## Page 4

Questions about this bulletin may be directed to the Market Conduct Division.
