## Page 12

Open: the claim has an active reserve. Closed: the claim is resolved with payment.

## Page 13

Suspended - litigation hold: the claim is open but all activity is paused by counsel. Use status code SUSPENDED.

A claim is litigated when a complaint has been filed against the insured.
