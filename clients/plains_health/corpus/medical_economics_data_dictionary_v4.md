## Page 1

This dictionary defines the measures in the medical economics dashboards: PMPM by service category (inpatient, outpatient, professional, pharmacy), utilization per 1,000 and unit cost.

Member months count members eligible on the first day of the month.

## Page 2

Service categories follow the claim: inpatient facility, outpatient facility, professional and pharmacy. Emergency physician services are professional.

## Page 3

PMPM is reported on an incurred basis: each claim is assigned to the month the service was provided, and a month is reported as complete once it has three months of runout. Months with less than three months of runout are labelled preliminary and are not used for trend.

Paid-basis views, which assign claims to the month they were paid, are used only for cash forecasting.

## Page 4

Unit cost is paid per admit for inpatient and paid per visit for professional services. Utilization is annualized per 1,000 members.
