## Page 1

This policy sets out when Plains covers hospital observation services, how long observation may last before a decision to admit or discharge, and how observation is reported.

Observation is a short period of assessment in a hospital bed to decide whether a member needs an inpatient admission. It is ordered by a physician and usually lasts less than 48 hours.

## Page 2

Observation services are outpatient services, whatever their length and even when the hospital bills them on an institutional claim with a diagnosis-related group. A hospital stay that began as observation and did not convert to an inpatient admission is reported as outpatient, not as an inpatient admission, in all cost and utilization reporting.

Medical economics applies this definition from the 2026 second-quarter reporting cycle and restates prior periods, so that inpatient admits per 1,000 and inpatient PMPM are comparable across hospitals that bill observation differently.

## Page 3

Observation beyond 48 hours requires a medical necessity review. Hospitals must give members the notice of observation status required by their state.
