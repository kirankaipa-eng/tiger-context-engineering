## Page 1

Modifier 25 marks a significant, separately identifiable evaluation and management service on the same day as a procedure. Modifier 59 marks a distinct procedural service. Both are legitimate and both are frequent sources of overpayment.

## Page 2

Modifier use is monitored for each rendering provider as the share of the provider's professional claims that carry the modifier, compared with the same share for the provider's peer cohort: providers of the same specialty practising in the same state. A provider whose rate is more than twice the cohort rate in two consecutive quarters is referred for prepayment review.

Payment integrity reports this ratio to the cohort quarterly for every provider with at least 50 professional claims in the quarter.

## Page 3

Referral is not a finding of fraud. Providers are notified and may submit documentation before any review begins.
