## Page 1

Several regional health plans say they will start reporting observation stays as outpatient care, after hospitals in some markets began billing more short stays on inpatient claims.

Analysts expect inpatient admission rates reported by plans to fall by 10% to 20% where the change is applied, with no change in the care members receive.
