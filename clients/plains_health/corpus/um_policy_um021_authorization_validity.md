## Page 1

This policy defines how long an approved prior authorization remains valid and how authorizations that are not used are recorded.

## Page 2

An approved authorization that is not used within 90 days of approval expires. Expired authorizations are recorded with their own status, expired, separate from requests the provider withdrew, so that approval rates and authorization-to-service conversion are not distorted.

Reporting that combines expired and withdrawn requests overstates provider withdrawals and understates unused approvals.

## Page 3

A provider may request an extension before expiry. Extensions are recorded as a new authorization linked to the original request.
