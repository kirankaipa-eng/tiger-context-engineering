## Page 1

Lapse reporting. Finance's quarterly deck shows a life lapse rate of 7.9% for 2025, computed as lapses during the year divided by policies in force on January 1. The Actuarial dashboard shows 5.9% from the governed lapse rate metric, which divides lapses by policy years of exposure. Committee members asked which one is "the" lapse rate for the board and for the marketing organizations' persistency scorecards.

No decision was reached. The committee asked the data owners for Finance and Actuarial to agree one definition, or to publish both under different names.
