## Page 1

Article 3, Retention and cessions. Northwind retains up to $1,000,000 of net amount at risk on any one life across all policies. Amounts in excess of retention on policies issued on or after January 1, 2026 are ceded automatically to the reinsurer on a yearly renewable term basis, up to the automatic binding limit.

Policies issued before 2026 remain ceded under the prior treaty, which closes to new business on December 31, 2025.

## Page 2

Article 7, Reporting. Northwind shall report monthly, for each ceded policy: the policy number, the insured, the amount retained, the net amount at risk ceded, the reinsurance premium and the treaty under which it is ceded, and shall report each claim on a ceded policy with the reinsurer's share.

Data note from Northwind Reinsurance Administration: today cessions are tracked in a spreadsheet outside the enterprise data model, which has no concept of a reinsurance cession. A cession record linking a policy to a treaty and reinsurer, with retained and ceded amounts and the reinsurance premium, is needed for net-of-reinsurance reporting.

## Page 3

Article 9, Recapture. Northwind may recapture cessions after the tenth policy year if its retention limit increases. Recapture is not expected in 2026.
