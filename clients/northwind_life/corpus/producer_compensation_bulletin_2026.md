## Page 1

Starting with business issued in 2026, Northwind will pay a persistency bonus to independent producers whose life business persists. The bonus is 10% of first-year commission, paid at month 13 when the producer's placed policies show 13-month persistency of 90% or better.

The bonus applies to Northwind's independent channel only. Career agents are covered by the agency's own incentive plan.

## Page 2

Commission records. Bonus payments are paid through the commission system as bonus commission. Each bonus payment record will carry the code of the Northwind program that generated it (PB26 for this program), because other bonus programs run at the same time and must be reconciled separately.

This program code is Northwind's own; other carriers do not use it. Please add it to Northwind's commission records in the enterprise data model.

## Page 3

Chargebacks on first-year commission for policies lapsing within twelve months are unchanged.
