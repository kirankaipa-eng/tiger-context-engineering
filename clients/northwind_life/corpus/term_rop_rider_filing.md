## Page 1

Northwind will offer an optional Return of Premium (ROP) rider on Northwind Term 10, Term 20 and Term 30 for policies issued on or after January 1, 2026. If the insured is living at the end of the level term period and the policy is in force, the rider refunds the base premiums paid.

The rider has its own charge, expressed as a percentage of the base premium, and is available at issue only. Partial refunds apply on surrender after year 15 of a Term 20 or Term 30 policy, per the schedule in the rider form.

## Page 2

Administration. The life policy administration system records the rider with rider code ROP on the policy's rider records. The rider amount is the total base premium that would be refunded at the end of the level term period.

The enterprise rider type list does not include a return-of-premium benefit. Please add it, so that ROP riders conform and rider counts and charges can be reported by type.

## Page 3

Pricing expects about a quarter of new term applicants to elect the rider. Commission on the rider charge follows the base policy schedule.
