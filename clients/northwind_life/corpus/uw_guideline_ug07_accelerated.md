## Page 1

Accelerated underwriting decides eligible term and IUL applications from electronic data sources (prescription history, medical information bureau, motor vehicle records, credit-based mortality score) without an exam or fluids.

Effective June 1, 2026, the eligibility limits change: issue ages 18 to 60 (unchanged); face amount up to $2,000,000 (was $1,000,000). Applicants who do not qualify are moved to full underwriting without a new application.

## Page 2

Accelerated decisions are limited to preferred plus through standard classes. Table-rated and declined decisions are always made by an underwriter on the full path. Random holdout audits continue at 5% of accelerated approvals.

## Page 3

Producers should not tell applicants which path applies before the case is submitted.
