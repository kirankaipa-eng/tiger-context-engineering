## Page 1

The Department adopted the NAIC Suitability in Annuity Transactions Model Regulation (#275, 2020 revision) in 2021. Under it, a producer recommending an annuity must act in the consumer's best interest, and the insurer must maintain a supervision system that reviews each recommendation before the contract is issued.

This bulletin announces amendments that take effect for applications signed on or after July 1, 2026. The amendments clarify the documentation insurers must keep. They do not change who is subject to the regulation.

## Page 2

Documentation of the basis for the recommendation. For every recommended annuity, the insurer's supervision record must show whether the producer documented the alternatives considered (other products, other strategies within the product, or no purchase) and why the recommended annuity was selected over them.

The record must carry this as a distinct, auditable item for each review, separate from the outcome of the review and separate from whether the consumer profile is complete. A review approved with an incomplete basis-of-recommendation record will be treated as a supervision failure in market conduct examinations.

Replacements. Where the sale replaces an existing annuity or life policy, the existing requirements for replacement disclosure and comparison continue to apply unchanged.

Training. Producers must continue to complete the best-interest training course required by the regulation before soliciting annuities. Insurers must verify completion; the Department does not require a new training record.

Questions may be directed to the Market Regulation Division.
