## Page 1

Annuity sales remained strong in the first quarter but continued to cool from the record levels of 2023 and 2024, as falling interest rates reduced the appeal of multi-year guaranteed annuities. Fixed indexed and registered index-linked annuities held up better, helped by equity markets.

Carriers said they expect more surrenders of contracts bought during the rate spike as their guarantee periods end, and are adjusting caps and declared rates on new business.

## Page 2

Analysts expect consolidation among marketing organizations to continue, and more carriers to offer index strategies that credit a fixed amount when the index is flat or up.
