## Page 1

Star Ratings run from 1 to 5 stars at the contract level and drive the quality bonus and rebate percentage. This memo summarizes the measure changes the Quality team is planning for.

## Page 2

Two measures move to the display page: they continue to be reported to CMS and published, but they no longer count toward the summary rating. A new survey measure, "Care Coordination Follow-up", is introduced on the display page first and is expected to become a rated measure two years later.

Our measure reference data must therefore distinguish rated measures from display-only measures. Today every measure in the model is treated as rated, so display measures would wrongly enter the weighted star average. Please add a display-only indicator to the Star measure definition.

## Page 3

Measure weights for the remaining measures are unchanged for planning purposes. Contract-level results continue to be loaded from the MA platform's star result extract.
