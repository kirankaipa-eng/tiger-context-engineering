## Page 1

Purpose: to confirm that the diagnoses that drive an inpatient MS-DRG are supported by the clinical record. This is distinct from coding validation, which checks that codes follow coding guidelines.

Scope: Medicare Advantage inpatient claims paid under MS-DRG contracts. Commercial claims are out of scope for 2026.

## Page 2

Effective March 1, 2026, inpatient claims selected for clinical validation that are not supported by the record are denied or re-priced to the supported DRG.

Denials from this review are reported under a new denial reason group, "Clinical validation", so that they can be tracked separately from medical necessity and coding denials. The MA claims platform sends the group code CLINICAL_VALIDATION on the claim header.

## Page 3

Providers may appeal clinical validation denials through the standard provider dispute process. Payment Integrity reports volumes and overturn rates monthly to the Provider Relations committee.
