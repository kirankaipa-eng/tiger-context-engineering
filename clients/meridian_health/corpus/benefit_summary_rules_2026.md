## Page 1

For the 2026 plan year, the specialist office visit copay on Small Group Gold PPO increases from $35 to $40. Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) continues to require prior authorization on all commercial plans.

## Page 2

Physical therapy visits are limited to 30 per plan year, combined with occupational therapy. Visits beyond the limit require medical necessity review. Benefit configuration will apply these rules in the claims platform's benefit tables.
