## Page 1

This policy describes how Meridian reviews prior authorization requests, including requests reviewed by delegated benefit managers for imaging, musculoskeletal and oncology services.

All requests are decided within 72 hours (expedited) or 7 calendar days (standard) of receipt. Delegates report every decision with its receipt and decision timestamps.

## Page 2

Delegation oversight reviews each delegate's timeliness monthly. A delegate whose standard-request timeliness falls below 95% in any month must submit a corrective action plan within 30 days.

## Page 3

Exemption ("gold card") program: from July 1, 2026, a practitioner whose requests for a service category were approved at least 92% of the time over the prior 12 months is exempt from prior authorization for that category for the next 12 months.

Each authorization record must indicate whether the request was waived under the exemption program, so that approval rates and timeliness are not distorted by exempt services. This is a Meridian program, not a regulatory requirement.
