## Page 1

Medicare Advantage insurers told analysts this quarter that Star Ratings remain a key driver of margins, as quality bonus payments and rebates depend on reaching four stars. Several executives said they are investing in medication adherence programs and member experience.

Analysts expect continued pressure on utilization trend, particularly outpatient surgery and post-acute care, and note that risk model changes have reduced revenue growth for some plans.
