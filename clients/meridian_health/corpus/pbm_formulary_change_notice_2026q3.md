## Page 1

Effective July 1, 2026, the following changes apply to the Meridian Medicare Part D formulary. Members affected by a tier change will receive notice at least 30 days in advance.

An adalimumab biosimilar product is added to the specialty tier as the preferred product. The reference adalimumab product remains on formulary with prior authorization required for new starts.

## Page 2

Beginning with the July claims files, our drug reference feed classifies each product as brand, generic or biosimilar. Biosimilars were previously reported as brand.

Plan sponsors that report generic dispensing or biosimilar uptake should add the biosimilar classification to their drug model; mapping biosimilars to brand will understate biosimilar uptake.

## Page 3

No changes to pharmacy network, dispensing fees or member cost-sharing amounts are included in this notice.
