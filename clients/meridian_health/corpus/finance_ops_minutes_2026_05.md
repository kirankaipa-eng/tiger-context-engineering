Attendees reviewed the April dashboard. The CFO asked why the "MCR" on the finance deck (82%) differs from the actuarial MLR (85%). Finance computes MCR as medical and pharmacy claims over all revenue, including ASO administrative fees and excluding quality improvement expense; the actuarial MLR excludes ASO and includes quality improvement. No decision was taken on which definition the board deck should use.
