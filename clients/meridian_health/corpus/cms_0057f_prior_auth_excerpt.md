## Page 1

This summary covers the CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule (CMS-0057-F) as it applies to Meridian Health Plan. It is written for utilization management, delegation oversight and analytics staff.

The rule has two parts: operational requirements that began in 2026 and API requirements that follow in 2027. This summary covers the operational part.

## Page 2

Impacted payers include Medicare Advantage organizations, state Medicaid and CHIP programs and managed care plans, and qualified health plan issuers on the federally facilitated exchanges.

Meridian's MA contracts are in scope. Commercial employer group business is not directly in scope, but the UM committee has decided to hold commercial requests to the same standard.

## Page 3

Beginning January 1, 2026, impacted payers must send prior authorization decisions within 72 hours for expedited (urgent) requests and within 7 calendar days for standard (non-urgent) requests.

The clock starts when the request is received. Analytics must therefore measure timeliness in hours from the receipt timestamp, not from the date the case was keyed, and report the share of decisions made within the applicable time frame as a governed metric.

## Page 4

When a request is denied, the notice must include a specific reason for the denial, regardless of how the request was submitted.

Payers must also publicly report prior authorization metrics annually, including approval and denial rates and average decision times.

## Page 5

Delegated utilization management does not move the obligation. Decisions made by a delegated benefit manager count as Meridian's decisions.
