## Page 1

This Amendment 3 modifies the primary care capitation terms of the Medicare Advantage Agreement between Meridian Health Plan, Inc. and Lakeshore Physician Partners, effective July 1, 2026.

## Page 2

Section 4.1 is replaced: the monthly primary care capitation rate increases from $165.00 to $172.00 per attributed member per month.

New Section 4.3, Quality Withhold: Meridian will withhold 5% of each monthly capitation payment. The withheld amounts are returned annually if Lakeshore meets the quality targets in Exhibit Q. Capitation reporting must show the gross capitation, the withhold rate and the net amount paid.

## Page 3

All other terms of the Agreement remain in effect. Exhibit Q (quality targets) is attached separately.
